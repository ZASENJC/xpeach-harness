# XPeach Harness Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

These rules keep the XPeach ecosystem healthy, avoid confusing users, and make related resources
easy to find. Please follow them.

## Names

- Product name: **XPeach Harness**, command line entry point `xph`.
- Owning site: **🍑Code** ([xpeach.codes](https://xpeach.codes)), an OpenAI-compatible relay.
- Always write the full name "XPeach Harness"; do not abbreviate it into a form that could be
  confused with another harness.

## Marks

- The 🍑 icon and the "🍑Code" wordmark identify this product.
- Do not alter, stretch, recolor, or combine them with other graphics.
- Do not use the marks in any way that implies official endorsement of a third-party build,
  mirror, or merchandise.

## Distribution

- Official installers are published only through
  [Releases](https://github.com/yellowpeachxgp/xpeach-harness/releases).
- A redistribution must state its origin and keep these guidelines and the [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Do not publish modified builds under the "XPeach" or "🍑Code" name.

## Relationship to upstream

This product reuses the DeepSeek Harness plugin core while keeping its own brand, data directory,
credential chain, and release pipeline. When you refer to upstream, use its own name and do not
mix upstream branding with this product's marks.
